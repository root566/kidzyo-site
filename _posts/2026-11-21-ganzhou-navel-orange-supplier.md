Finding the right fruit supplier starts with a clear picture of your needs. Before requesting a quote, consider the quantity, destination, preferred fruit size, packaging requirements, and delivery window.

## Questions to ask a supplier

Ask about the current harvest season, product specifications, available packaging, minimum order quantity, and how fruit is stored and transported. For international shipments, confirm which documents and inspection requirements apply to your destination with the supplier and relevant authorities.
