This is an example article for the new Kidzyo Journal. It shows how a story appears on its own page and how readers can find it from the homepage.

## A place for useful ideas

The journal can grow around the topics that matter to its readers: practical guides, fresh perspectives, and helpful resources. Each article can have its own title, date, category, and introduction.

## What comes next

This example can be replaced with original content once the subject of the site is chosen. New posts can be published by adding a Markdown file to the website's GitHub repository.
